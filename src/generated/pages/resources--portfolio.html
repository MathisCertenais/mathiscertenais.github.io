Building a Resume

Writing a resume is hard—especially in a competitive field like product design. Between telling a clear story, standing out to hiring teams, and making sure your resume is ATS-friendly, it’s easy to feel stuck.

Rezi is a AI-based resume builder. Normally $30/month, one can get a free lifetime account through the instructions on this reddit post.

Writing Case Studies

NotebookLM is an AI-powered research and note-taking assistant built by Google Labs that helps you understand, organize, and interact with your own documents and knowledge sources using artificial intelligence. While originally developed as a research tool, its ability to generate slide shows is an incredible tool for building case studies.

Consider writing a rough outline of your case study, submitting it to NotebookLM, and using its output as the foundation for your polished, public-ready iteration.
