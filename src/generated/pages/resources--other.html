Automate Applying for Jobs

Simplify.jobs is an AI-powered job search platform designed to help job seekers streamline and automate much of the job hunting process. When using their free browser extension, the amount of manual effort needed when applying for jobs is greatly diminished.

Practice Networking

Lunchclub is an online professional networking platform that connects people for one-on-one meetings. I set this up to meet a new person every Thursday.

While this isn’t a service for getting leads, it’s a great service for practicing talking with other business professionals and your networking skills.

Lunchclub is currently invite-only, however, you can sign up using my personal referral link.

Stay Inspired with Panda

Panda is a free Chrome extension that turns every new tab into a curated feed of the best design, product, and tech reading. Sources like Dribbble, Designer News, Smashing, and Product Hunt land in one place, so staying current becomes a side-effect of your normal browsing instead of another tab to babysit.

Install Panda from their site, or grab it directly on the Chrome Web Store.

Join a Design Community

Design Buddies is a free, global, community-driven design network centered around a highly active Discord server where designers of all levels connect, share work, and support one another’s growth. It offers portfolio feedback, career advice, events, and peer mentorship in a welcoming, collaborative environment focused on learning and professional development.
